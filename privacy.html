Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

GIFStar is designed to be stateless. Submitted images are processed for conversion and are not intentionally saved by GIFStar to a database or object-storage service.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GIFStar handles information when you use the application. GIFStar is an open-source Discord utility maintained by Eisenrot.

The application's purpose is narrow: receive an image you explicitly provide through Discord, convert it into a GIF, and return the resulting file to Discord.

2. Information GIFStar processes

When you invoke /gifstar, Discord sends an interaction request to GIFStar. Depending on the context, that request can include:

  • the image attachment you explicitly selected, including its Discord-hosted URL, filename, size, and content type;
  • Discord interaction metadata needed to authenticate and answer the request, such as application, interaction, user, channel, or server identifiers;
  • the interaction token used to send the converted file back to Discord;
  • technical request information that may be handled by the hosting infrastructure, such as timestamps, network information, request status, and basic runtime logs.

GIFStar does not ask you to create a separate account and does not request passwords, payment information, address-book data, or Discord message history.

3. How the information is used

The information above is used only as needed to:

  • verify that a request genuinely came from Discord;
  • download and decode the image you chose;
  • convert the image into GIF format;
  • respect Discord's attachment-size limit for the interaction;
  • return the result or an error message to Discord;
  • maintain security, reliability, and diagnose technical failures.

4. Image storage and retention

GIFStar processes submitted image data in memory while carrying out the conversion. The application does not intentionally persist submitted images to an application database, filesystem archive, or object-storage bucket.

After the response is sent, GIFStar does not maintain its own copy of the source or converted image. Discord may retain attachments and messages according to Discord's own systems, settings, and policies.

Hosting and platform providers may temporarily process or retain limited technical logs in accordance with their own operational and security practices. GIFStar does not use those logs to build advertising profiles or track users across services.

5. Logging

GIFStar may emit technical error logs when a conversion fails or when Discord rejects a response. These logs are intended for debugging and service reliability. The application does not intentionally log the binary contents of submitted images.

6. Sharing and third parties

GIFStar does not sell personal information and does not share information with advertisers or data brokers.

The service necessarily relies on:

  • Discord, which delivers interaction data and attachments and receives GIFStar's responses. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel, which hosts and executes GIFStar's serverless application. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, third parties, or legal rights.

7. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

GIFStar itself does not use advertising trackers, behavioral advertising, or a user analytics database. The legal pages do not intentionally set application cookies.

Discord and Vercel may process technical information under their own policies when you use their platforms or infrastructure.

8. Security

GIFStar verifies Discord interaction signatures before accepting commands and applies limits to source file size, decoded image size, dimensions, download time, and Discord output size. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but the application is designed to minimize retained data and unnecessary access.

9. International processing

Discord and Vercel operate infrastructure in multiple countries. As a result, information needed to provide the service may be processed outside your country of residence subject to the safeguards and terms used by those providers.

10. Your choices and rights

You can stop GIFStar from processing future information by simply not invoking the application or by removing it from your Discord account.

Because GIFStar does not maintain user accounts or an application database of submitted images, it generally has no persistent user record to retrieve or delete. If you believe information concerning you has been retained or you have a privacy request under applicable law, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub repository. Requests relating to data held by Discord or Vercel should be directed to those providers.

11. Children's privacy

GIFStar is not intended to expand access to Discord beyond Discord's own age and eligibility requirements. Do not use GIFStar if you are not permitted to use Discord in your jurisdiction.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when GIFStar's functionality, hosting, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.

13. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to the maintainer through the GIFStar GitHub repository.
